# Misty
A narrative-driven adventure focused on psychological exploration, developed in **Unreal Engine** for **VR**. <br>
The game features fully voiced characters using our own voices, with **audio dialogues accompanied by subtitles**, carefully adapted to ensure an immersive and comfortable **virtual reality experience**.
The experience also includes **two mini-games** that enrich the gameplay and narrative progression.
<br>

# Technical System
- Game Engine: Unreal Engine <br>

- Headset Type: MetaQuest 3 <br>

- Target Platforms: Oculus


# Lore
the player is plunged into a coma following recent events. Guided by his cat Misty during his coma, he relives a recent memory of spending an evening with his group of friends around a campfire in the forest. During this dream, he talks and interacts with them until he witnesses a problematic situation. Faced with this revelation, the player must confront a moral dilemma that will jeopardize the relationships within the group.
